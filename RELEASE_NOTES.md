# Wee Orchestrator for macOS v0.9.0

Released July 27, 2026.

## New

- The workspace rail on the left can now collapse to an icon-only strip, either
  from its toggle button or with Cmd+\. Nothing is hidden when it collapses:
  the health dot, environment picker, refresh, update check, and section badges
  all stay reachable as icons with tooltips, so the space goes to your content
  rather than to labels. The choice persists across launches.
- Cmd+= and Cmd+- now increase and decrease the client text size from anywhere
  in the app, mirroring the Increase/Decrease controls in Appearance settings.
  These are the standard macOS zoom bindings, so they behave the way they do in
  a browser.
